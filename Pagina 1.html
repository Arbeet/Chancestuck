And there they sat. Eyes glued to the flickering lights, stacks of lusus energy cans laying on the ground, and a bean bag sofa now perfectly molded to codexx's ass. A kind of caffeine and insomnia fueled symbiotic environment nothing could break apart.

Gog could come down and Codexx would never notice, so its no surprise they didnt realize a familiar pair of eyes was staring through their open door.
